About Norevio

Three principles we don't compromise on

Private by default

We only ask for what an app genuinely needs to work. Anything that can run entirely on the device is kept on the device.

Localised, not translated

Our apps speak the user's own language — EduGrade alone ships twenty locales — matched to local wording and local formats, not pushed through a machine translator.

Fewer things, done properly

Each app solves one clear problem all the way through, instead of piling on features.
